Demolition work in Tallinn and Harju County

Controlled demolition of apartments, bathrooms and smaller structures.

Proper and safe execution Clear quotation Tallinn and Harju County Send a description of the work and photos

For interior demolition and dismantling, we assess structures, access to the work area, dust control and the volume of waste sorting and removal. Load-bearing structures are not removed without the necessary design and approval.

Demolition must be controlled, safe and well planned, especially in apartment buildings and spaces undergoing renovation. We carry out interior demolition, remove old finishes, strip out bathrooms and arrange construction waste removal in Tallinn and Harju County.

Before demolition, we assess structures, utilities and access. This helps prevent damage to load-bearing elements, plumbing, electrics and adjoining rooms.

What does the service include?

  • removal of old tiles, parquet, plasterboard and finishes
  • interior demolition in bathrooms, kitchens and apartments
  • removal of smaller partitions and structures
  • sorting construction waste and arranging its removal

When should you book this service?

Demolition is usually commissioned before bathroom renovation, a major apartment renovation or installation of new flooring and walls. Proper demolition speeds up subsequent work and reduces unexpected additional costs.

Work process

Site inspection

We check what can be removed and which elements must be retained or assessed separately by a specialist.

Protection and demolition

We cover surfaces that will remain, isolate the work area where needed and carry out demolition in a sequence that keeps dust and risks under control.

Cleaning

We collect waste, sort materials and leave the surface ready for the next construction stages.

Demolition costs and safety

Demolition costs depend on the volume of materials to be removed, floor level, access, waste removal and whether the work is in an apartment building or a detached house. Before work begins, the locations of pipes, electrical cables and structures that must not be damaged need to be established.

During the work, we keep retained surfaces protected and aim to limit dust spread. After demolition, it is important that the site is in a suitable condition for the next stages: the substrate is exposed, hazardous remnants are removed and the work area is cleaned.

Local experience and agreeing the scope of work

In apartment buildings, demolition must take account of neighbours, stairwells and waste removal routes to keep the work controlled and safe. When assessing an enquiry, we always check site access, whether the space is occupied, which surfaces need protection and the most practical sequence of work. This helps avoid having to reopen or repair a completed stage later.

Before starting, we provide a clear, realistic estimate. If photos are not enough, we arrange a site visit. This lets us agree on materials, timing, related work and potential risks before the project begins.

Before demolition in an apartment, it is useful to agree which elements will remain: doors, windows, pipes, flooring or furniture. This reduces the risk of mistakes and helps the team distinguish layers to be removed from surfaces to be retained.

Demolition prices

Demolition costs depend on the volume of material to be removed, floor level, access, dust protection and waste removal. Apartment buildings also require consideration of working hours, stairwell protection and waste removal routes.

For a faster quotation, please include photos, measurements, the site location and your preferred deadline where possible. This helps us determine whether a site visit is needed or an initial estimate can be based on your description.

Practical information about demolition work

Materials and waste

Demolition requires separating construction rubble, timber, metal, tiles, sanitary ware and potentially hazardous materials. Correct sorting makes disposal clearer and reduces problems on site later.

Time required

The time required depends on the type of structure, floor level, access, waste volume and whether the work is in an occupied apartment or an empty property.

Pricing factors

Costs are affected by waste removal, protective measures, dust control, avoiding load-bearing structures, the presence of plumbing or electrics, and parking or lift access.

Common mistakes

Common mistakes include starting demolition without checking utilities, misidentifying a load-bearing wall and leaving waste removal routes unprotected.

Advice for customers

Before demolition, agree what will remain, what will be disposed of and which surfaces need protection. In an apartment building, building rules and permitted working hours must also be considered.

Demolition boundaries, building systems and waste

Load-bearing or non-load-bearing wall

A wall's function cannot be determined by thickness or tapping alone. Before removal, drawings are checked and a structural specialist's assessment is obtained where necessary. Altering a load-bearing element or creating an opening requires a suitable design and the required approvals.

Electrical, water and ventilation connections

Before work begins, we identify concealed cables and pipes and arrange the necessary disconnections with a qualified specialist. In apartment buildings, we consider shared systems, working hours and access. Retained surfaces and access routes are protected.

Asbestos and waste sorting

The composition of old boards, pipe insulation or coverings cannot always be identified visually. If asbestos is suspected, the material is not broken up or sanded; work stops for inspection and appropriate disposal arrangements. Masonry rubble, timber, metal and hazardous waste are separated according to the receiving facility's requirements.

Work arrangements before and after demolition

We define the scope of work

We mark elements to be removed and retained, agree how to reduce dust spread and determine the waste removal route. The price also takes account of floor level, lift use, parking and skip placement.

We demolish in a controlled sequence

We remove layers to the agreed extent while monitoring retained structures and building systems. If unexpected damage or an unfamiliar material is found, we clarify the solution before continuing.

We leave a suitable surface for the next stage

We clean to the agreed extent and check the exposed substrate. Levelling, new pipes and electrical work are not automatically included in the demolition price: related work is set out in the quotation.

For a quotation: Provide photos, dimensions of the elements to be removed, the floor level and information about lift and waste removal access. Indicate known building systems and any existing designs.

Frequently asked questions

Do you remove construction waste?

We can arrange waste collection and removal according to the scope of work and our agreement.

Can demolition be carried out in an apartment building?

Yes, but we take account of building rules, permitted noise hours, access and neighbours.

Can load-bearing walls be removed?

Alterations to load-bearing walls require a design and approvals. We do not undertake such work without the necessary documentation.

Do you provide dust protection?

Yes, where needed we cover passageways and surfaces that will remain, and isolate the work area.

Can I live in the apartment during demolition?

This may be possible for smaller jobs, but dust, noise and access make it inconvenient. It is better to vacate the space during work in wet areas or major demolition.

Is waste removal included?

This must be agreed separately in the quotation. Waste volume, floor level, lift access and parking significantly affect removal costs.

How do you limit the spread of dust?

We use protective coverings, isolate areas where needed and follow a work sequence that reduces dust spreading into other rooms. Completely dust-free demolition is not realistic.

Can a load-bearing wall be demolished?

Altering a load-bearing wall requires a design and approvals. A load-bearing wall is not demolished without a structural assessment.

How do you ensure demolition safety?

Before demolition, we clarify which elements will be removed, protect surfaces that will remain and agree on waste handling and work arrangements. Alterations to load-bearing structures require a design and approvals.

Which other jobs are demolition works usually linked to?

Demolition gives a renovation a clean starting point, but it should be considered alongside the other stages. After old finishes are removed, work often moves on to building new walls or ceilings, in bathrooms to substrate preparation and tiling and on floors to installing new parquet or laminate. The overall budget can be assessed using the renovation price list.
