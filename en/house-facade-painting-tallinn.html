House facade painting in Tallinn

An example of a completed exterior facade refresh.

This completed project shows how a timber facade is cleaned before painting, damage is repaired, the surface is primed and access and paint coats are planned.

Project description

Facade painting is visible work, but its durability depends on preparation. In Tallinn and Harju County, the result is affected by weather, facade material, moisture and the condition of the old paint.

Initial condition

An ageing facade may have dirt, loose paint, sun damage or signs of moisture. If these areas are not addressed before painting, the new finish will not last long enough.

Solution

The surface is cleaned, loose paint is removed and problem areas are primed before the facade is painted in suitable weather. Scaffolding or another safe means of access is used where necessary.

Why does this matter?

These project descriptions help customers understand the preparation and sequence of work a similar site needs. Photos and descriptions of the stages help you assess the desired result and plan your own project.

Facade painting: the work process

Facade painting in Tallinn always starts with assessing the surface condition, because timber facades, rendered facades and previously painted cladding require different preparation. The aim of this completed project was to restore the appearance of a house so that the new finish would protect the surface from weather, UV radiation and moisture. The work involves more than adding a coat of paint: facade washing, removing loose paint, checking damaged areas, priming and using a suitable facade paint are all important.

We carry out facade work in stages. First, we clean dirt and dust from the surface and check connections, areas around windows, corners and places where water may collect. We repair peeling paint and uneven areas before priming and painting.

What should you send to request a quote for similar work?

For a more accurate facade painting quote, send photos of every side of the house, the approximate facade area, details of the condition of the old paint and the address or area. If the property requires scaffolding, this affects the cost and schedule. It is also important to know whether you want painting only or also facade repairs, cladding replacement, joint checks, window reveal finishing or plinth maintenance.

Frequently asked question

Can a facade be painted immediately after washing?

Not necessarily. The surface must be sufficiently dry before painting, and loose layers must be removed.
