Renovation services in one place

We help when your home, apartment, house or commercial premises need a carefully planned renovation. Our services include interior finishing, tiling, painting, drywall work, parquet installation, wallpapering, wall panel installation, LED lighting solutions, demolition and a range of exterior works.

We work mainly in Tallinn and Harju County. Before starting, we clarify the property's condition, the desired result, materials and deadlines. If the work involves several stages, such as demolition, drywall, painting and flooring, we plan the sequence so that one stage does not damage another.

Interior finishing and renovation

We carry out painting work, tiling, drywall work, parquet installation and wallpapering. These services are suitable for refreshing a single room or completely renovating an apartment.

Exterior work and maintenance

For exterior work, we help with facade work, façade painting, roofing, concrete work, foundation work and snow removal.

How do we work?

A good renovation starts with a clear description. It is helpful to include photos, room dimensions, the address or area and the desired result in your enquiry. This helps assess whether a site inspection is needed, which materials are suitable and what sequence of work makes sense.

A clear sequence of work reduces risks

Renovation problems most often arise when the sequence of work has not been thought through. Before starting, we therefore review which tasks depend on each other, when drying time is needed, when materials can be delivered and how to protect finished surfaces.

If a project includes several services, we help divide the work into stages. This makes it easier to estimate the budget, plan materials and decide which tasks need to be completed before the final finishes.

Frequently asked questions

Which services can I order in one place?

You can order interior finishing, tiling, painting, drywall work, parquet installation, LED lighting solutions, demolition, façade work, roofing and seasonal snow removal in one place.

Do you only work in Tallinn?

Our main service area is Tallinn and Harju County. For larger projects, we also consider work in other areas.

What should I include in an enquiry?

Include a description of the work, the location, approximate dimensions, your preferred deadline and photos if possible. This helps us assess the scope of work more quickly.
