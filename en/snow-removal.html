Snow removal and roof snow clearing

Snow clearing from roofs, entrances and areas around buildings in Tallinn and Harju County.

Experienced team and agreed working arrangements Clear quotation Tallinn and Harju County Send a description of the work and photos

For snow clearing on a roof, in a yard or around a building, we assess the hazard area, snow volume, access and suitable equipment. Before roof work starts, a safe working method and snow drop zone are established.

Snow removal helps reduce the risks of icicles, excessive snow loads and slippery surfaces. We clear snow from roofs and around buildings and carry out work to reduce slipperiness in Tallinn and Harju County.

Unlike the roofing page, this page focuses on prompt winter maintenance. Where needed, we also note visible problems with the roof or gutters during the work, but repairs and maintenance belong to our roofing service.

What does the service include?

  • roof snow clearing and icicle removal
  • clearing entrances, pavements and areas around buildings
  • ice control and marking hazardous areas
  • seasonal maintenance for apartment associations and private owners

When should you book this service?

Snow removal is usually needed urgently after heavy snowfall, a thaw or icicle formation. Timely action reduces damage to the building and danger to passers-by.

Work process

Safety assessment

We assess access, roof shape, snow volume, icicles and people's movements around the work area.

Snow clearing

We remove snow and ice in a controlled way to avoid damaging roof coverings, gutters or facades.

Tidying the work area

We clear or mark areas of fallen snow and report visible problems.

Booking snow removal

Snow removal costs depend on snow volume, roof height, access, icicles and whether the work area needs to be cordoned off. For an urgent enquiry, photos of the hazardous area and the address are particularly useful for assessing access and the scope of work.

After snow clearing, we check for visible damage to gutters, roof coverings or facades. Repairs are not included in snow removal, but where needed we advise if the building subsequently requires a roofing or facade inspection.

Local experience and agreeing the scope of work

Speed matters in snow removal, but safety matters even more: the work area must be assessed for both the person on the roof and people below. When assessing an enquiry, we always check site access, whether the space is occupied, which surfaces need protection and the most practical sequence of work. This helps avoid having to reopen or repair a completed stage later.

Before starting, we provide a clear, realistic estimate. If photos are not enough, we arrange a site visit. This lets us agree on materials, timing, related work and potential risks before the project begins.

When booking snow removal, it is important to know where snow can be placed safely. When clearing roofs or entrances, we ensure removed snow does not block routes, damage landscaping or create a new slippery area.

Snow removal prices

Snow removal costs depend on snow volume, roof height, icicles, access and whether the work area must be cordoned off. For an urgent enquiry, provide photos and the address or area.

For a faster quotation, please include photos, measurements, the site location and your preferred deadline where possible. This helps us determine whether a site visit is needed or an initial estimate can be based on your description.

Practical information about snow removal

Tools and safety

Snow removal uses tools that do not damage roof coverings, facades or rainwater systems. Safety equipment and cordoning off the work area are important on taller buildings.

Time required

The time required depends on snow volume, ice, roof pitch, access and whether only hazardous edges or the entire surface need clearing.

Pricing factors

Costs are affected by building height, snow volume, ice removal, access, urgency and safety measures to protect pedestrians or cars.

Common mistakes

Common mistakes include damaging roof coverings with metal tools, pushing snow into dangerous areas and removing icicles without cordoning off the area below.

Advice for customers

If snow or ice threatens an entrance, pavement or parking area, give the precise location and include photos in your enquiry. Access information is especially important for urgent work.

Roof snow removal follows safety and roof material requirements

Load and situation assessment

Snow depth alone does not indicate the load: wet snow and ice can be much heavier than fresh dry snow. Unusual deflection, cracks or other structural signs require prompt specialist assessment. There is no universal safe depth in centimetres for all roofs.

Roof covering and equipment

The roof covering, pitch and access determine the working method. We use tools suitable for the surface and plan fall protection and anchor points. Ice is not indiscriminately struck against the roof covering: this can damage its finish, joints or watertightness.

People and the snow drop zone

Before work begins, the hazardous area is cordoned off and entrances, cars and pedestrians are taken into account. Falling snow, icicles and slippery surfaces can also endanger people beside the building. Roof clearing and removal of snow fallen to the ground are agreed separately.

Agreed sequence of work

Photos and access

We assess the roof, safe access to it and the area for placing snow. We clarify whether the work includes icicles, gutters or snow removal from the site.

Balanced removal

Work is planned according to the roof structure and covering, avoiding unjustified uneven loading. Where needed, a suitable layer of snow is left to protect the covering; the specific method is decided based on the on-site assessment.

Final checks and cleaning

We check the work area, entrances and agreed drainage points. New snowfall can quickly create a new load, so recurring maintenance requirements are agreed separately.

For a quotation: Send the building location, height, roof photos and access information. Describe whether the problem is snow load, icicles or blocked drainage, and whether you want snow removed from the site.

Frequently asked questions

Do you remove icicles?

Yes, where access and safety allow. We cordon off the work area where needed.

Do you work for apartment associations?

Yes, we help apartment associations with snow clearing on roofs and around buildings.

Does snow removal include roof repairs?

No, snow removal is seasonal cleaning. Roof repairs are covered by our roofing service.

How soon can you come?

This depends on weather and the work schedule. For an immediate hazard, include photos and the address in your enquiry.

When should snow be removed from a roof?

Snow should be removed when loads become excessive, icicles form or snow threatens people, cars and entrances. Dangerous situations should be dealt with promptly.

Can snow removal damage the roof covering?

Yes, with the wrong tools. We use methods that reduce the risk of damaging roof coverings, gutters and facades.

Do you also clear icicles?

Yes, where access and safety allow. Before starting, the area where snow or ice may fall must be cordoned off.

Can I book a one-off snow removal service?

Yes. You can book a one-off response to a hazardous situation or recurring maintenance during periods of heavy snowfall.

How do you ensure snow removal safety?

Before clearing snow, we assess access and the hazard area, agree on the scope and organise the work to protect people and property.

Which other jobs is snow removal usually linked to?

Snow removal is seasonal maintenance that helps protect the roof, facade and surroundings. When snow or ice accumulates on a roof, related work often includes roof condition checks and maintenance; for taller buildings, it is necessary to assess safe access requirements. After meltwater or ice damage, it may also be necessary to carry out a facade and plinth condition inspection.
