Roofing services in Tallinn and Harju County

Roof maintenance, repairs, washing, painting and renovation.

3-year warranty on completed construction and finishing work Clear quotation Tallinn and Harju County Send a description of the work and photos

When planning roof maintenance, washing or repairs, we assess the condition of the covering, access, safety equipment requirements and possible leaks. The scope of work and suitable solution are agreed before starting.

Roofing work starts with a condition assessment. We check the covering, penetrations, ridges, edge flashings, gutters and places where water may enter the structure. This helps determine whether maintenance, washing, local repairs, preparation for painting or more thorough renovation is needed.

We maintain and repair roofs in Tallinn and Harju County. Removing roof snow and ice is a separate seasonal safety service; here we focus on roof repairs, washing, maintenance and renovation planning.

What does the service include?

  • visual inspection of roof coverings, ridges, edges and penetrations
  • roof washing, moss and dirt removal and surface preparation
  • checks of minor leaks, fixings, seals and edge flashings
  • planning the sequence of roof painting, maintenance or renovation
  • assessment of safe access, scaffolding or access lift requirements

When should you book this service?

Roofing services are worth booking when a roof is mossy, paint or protective coatings are worn, gutters do not work properly, areas around penetrations need checking or there are signs of dampness or possible leaks indoors. Timely maintenance helps prevent more extensive moisture damage.

Work process

Inspection

We inspect the covering type, pitch, access, penetrations, gutters, ridges, fixings and visible damage.

Carrying out the work

We carry out the agreed cleaning, washing, preparation for painting, minor repairs or maintenance according to the roofing material and safety requirements.

Checks and recommendations

After the work, we explain which areas need monitoring, when the next maintenance should be carried out and whether the roof needs major repairs soon.

Assessing roof maintenance

Roofing costs depend on roof type, pitch, access, working height, extent of damage, safety equipment and whether scaffolding or an access lift is needed. Roof washing, painting, leak repairs and renovation preparation involve different amounts of work, so photos and a site description help refine the quotation.

During the work, we pay attention to penetrations, gutters, fixings, ridge flashings and places where water may start damaging the structure. Problems involving winter snow or icicles are handled under our separate snow removal service.

Local experience and agreeing the scope of work

Roof conditions vary greatly across Tallinn and Harju County: some need regular maintenance, some leak checks and others more thorough renovation planning. When assessing an enquiry, we always consider access, whether the building is residential or commercial, how rainwater drains and whether the work affects the facade, eaves or interior finishes.

We provide a clear, realistic assessment before starting. If photos are insufficient, we arrange a site inspection. This allows the scope, materials, safety measures, time required and potential risk areas to be clarified in advance.

Roof maintenance should be planned before periods of heavy rain and winter. Small leaks, blocked gutters or loose details that go unnoticed may later affect the facade, insulation and interior finishes.

Roofing prices

Roofing costs depend on roof type, pitch, access, the extent of washing or repairs and safety requirements. Photos of the roof, gutters and penetrations help assess the scope more accurately.

For a faster quotation, please include photos, measurements, the site location and your preferred deadline where possible. This helps us determine whether a site visit is needed or an initial estimate can be based on your description.

Practical information about roofing work

Materials

Roofing methods depend on the covering material. Metal roofs, tiled roofs, fibre-cement roofs, bituminous coverings and different penetrations require different cleaning, repairs and maintenance. Attention must be paid to fixings, seals, ridges, edge flashings and water flow.

Time required

The time required depends on roof pitch, access, weather, safety measures and the extent of damage. On a wet or icy roof, or in strong winds, rushing at the expense of quality is unwise.

Pricing factors

Costs are affected by roof area, height, safety equipment, the number of penetrations, rainwater systems and the need for scaffolding or an access lift.

Common mistakes

Common mistakes include postponing minor leak repairs, failing to check seals around penetrations and washing roofs in a way that damages the covering.

Advice for customers

Send photos of the problem area, the whole roof surface, gutters, penetrations and access. If the problem only occurs during rain, describe where water enters, when it happens and whether that location has been repaired before.

Roof covering, underlay and ventilation

Metal, tiles or rolled roofing

The suitability of a roof covering depends on pitch, the structure's load-bearing capacity, building shape and the existing solution. Profiled metal and standing-seam roof fixings and permitted pitches can differ. A heavier tiled roof requires checks of the supporting structure; flat roof layer arrangements require a separate design.

Underlay and airflow

Underlay safely drains water that gets beneath the covering. Breathable and non-breathable underlays are not installed in the same way: for example, Ruukki instructions separately address ventilation requirements on each side of the underlay. Eaves, ridge and penetration details must work together as a complete system.

Metal flashings and safety products

Junctions around chimneys, valleys and roof windows are frequent leak locations. We plan gutters, snow guards, walkways and roof ladders together with the roof. Safety product fixings must suit the specific covering and supporting structure.

Roof repair sequence

Inspection and temporary protection

We check the cause of the leak, structural condition and work area. We agree which part of the roof will be opened and the temporary weather protection so repairs do not expose living spaces to rain. The design and approvals are clarified where necessary.

Base and covering installation

We remove the agreed layers, repair damage and install underlay, ventilation layers and battens according to the chosen system. Covering overlaps and fixings are determined by the system requirements.

Junctions and drainage

We finish penetrations and edges, check the rainwater route and clean the work area. At handover, we clarify maintenance and safe access: snow guards and roof ladders still require periodic fixing inspections.

For a quotation: Send roof photos from outside and, if possible, from the attic, approximate dimensions and a description of the leak. Indicate the existing covering and whether you want a local repair or a complete roof replacement.

Frequently asked questions

Do you wash roofs?

Yes, we clean and wash roofs according to the condition of the roof covering.

Do you paint roofs?

Yes, we carry out roof painting work where the surface and preparation are suitable.

Is snow clearing included in the same service?

Snow clearing is a separate service so urgent winter work is clearly distinguished.

Is going onto the roof safe?

We work only when access and weather allow it to be done safely. We use safety equipment or scaffolding where needed.

Can a small leak be repaired without replacing the entire roof?

Often yes, if the damage is local and the roof is in good overall condition. If the problem recurs in several places, the need for more extensive renovation must be assessed.

Can washing damage a roof?

Yes, if the wrong pressure or method is used. Washing must suit the roofing material to avoid damaging the surface, fixings or protective coating.

Can roofing work be carried out in winter?

Some maintenance and emergency work is possible, but weather, snow, ice and safety limit its scope. Larger jobs are best planned for a more suitable season.

Do you also check rainwater gutters?

Yes, gutters and water flow are an important part of a roof inspection, because blockages or incorrect falls can cause facade and moisture damage.

Is completed work covered by a warranty?

Yes. Construction and finishing work completed by RK Meistrid OÜ is covered by a 3-year warranty. The scope of the work and agreed terms are recorded in the quotation.

Which other jobs are roofing works usually linked to?

Roofing work directly affects the facade, eaves and moisture protection of the building. When working at greater heights or on a steeper surface, it is often necessary to plan safe access and a working platform. At the junction of roof edges and the facade, work may be linked to exterior wall repairs or maintenance, and in winter to removal of snow and ice from roofs.
