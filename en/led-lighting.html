Choosing an LED strip, power supply and light quality

Colour temperature and colour rendering

Warm light often suits relaxation areas, while more neutral light suits work surfaces. The choice is made alongside the room's other lighting so that colour temperatures do not clash. LEDs with good colour rendering, for example CRI 90 or higher, help show material colours more naturally; we test the solution before final installation.

Profile, diffuser and power

An aluminium profile helps dissipate heat, and a diffuser softens the individual light points. A continuous, dot-free line depends on LED density, profile depth and the diffuser. The power supply is sized according to the strip's total power and the reserve specified by the manufacturer; long runs may need power feeds at several points.

Moisture and serviceability

The IP rating is chosen according to the location and moisture exposure. Bathroom electrical zones and connections require an electrician's assessment; a more waterproof strip does not make any power supply location suitable. The power supply, controller and connections must remain serviceable, and the dimmer must be compatible with the system.

For planning: Send the length of the area to be illuminated, photos of the room and your preferred control method. Specify whether you need task lighting, indirect lighting or a decorative line, and whether the existing power supply is suitable.

06 — GOOD TO KNOW

Before the
lights come on.

Answers to questions that help you plan your lighting solution.

Can LED strips be installed in a bathroom?

Yes, provided the components have suitable moisture protection and the connections are installed safely.

Does the power supply need to remain accessible?

Yes, the power supply must remain accessible for maintenance and possible replacement.

Do you also conceal the cables?

Where possible, yes. The best result is achieved when the wiring is planned before finishing work.

Which LED colour temperature should I choose?

Warm or neutral light often suits general lighting at home. Work surfaces may benefit from a more neutral light that does not distort colours.

Can LED lighting be added to an already renovated room?

Yes, but the solution depends on access to the cables and where the power supply can be concealed. The neatest result is achieved when lighting is planned before plasterboard and painting work is completed.

Can an LED strip provide the main lighting?

Sometimes, but an LED strip often works better as mood or indirect lighting. For main lighting, the room size, luminous flux and diffuser quality must be considered.

Where is the LED power supply installed?

The power supply must be in an accessible, ventilated location. It should not be completely enclosed in a way that makes later maintenance or replacement impossible.

Can LED lighting be connected to a dimmer?

Yes, if the LED strip, power supply and controls are compatible. Choose the dimmer to suit the system, rather than just the appearance of the switch.

Is completed work covered by a warranty?

Yes. Construction and finishing work completed by RK Meistrid OÜ is covered by a 3-year warranty. The scope of the work and agreed terms are recorded in the quotation.
