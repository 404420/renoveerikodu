Facade painting in Tallinn and Harju County

Washing, priming and durable painting of timber and rendered facades.

3-year warranty on completed construction and finishing work Clear quotation Tallinn and Harju County Send a description of the work and photos

The result of painting a timber or rendered facade depends on the substrate condition, preparation and the correct paint system. Before quoting, we assess the extent of repairs, access and the sequence of work.

Facade painting gives a house a fresh appearance and protects the surface against moisture, UV radiation and weather. We paint timber and rendered facades in Tallinn and Harju County.

Quality painting begins with washing, removing loose paint and priming. We select the paint system to suit the existing surface, as well as the desired colour.

What does the service include?

  • washing and preparation of timber and rendered facades
  • removal of loose paint and repair work
  • priming and weather-resistant painting
  • finishing of edges, window reveals and details

Work process

Cleaning

We wash or clean the surface, remove loose layers and allow it to dry sufficiently before painting.

Priming

We prime problem areas or absorbent patches so the paint adheres well and the colour remains even.

Painting

We paint in suitable weather conditions and check coverage, transitions and the finish of details.

Painting costs and weather

The cost of facade painting depends on the surface material, need for washing, removal of loose paint, priming, height and number of paint coats. Weather directly affects the work: an excessively wet, cold or hot surface can compromise adhesion and paint durability.

Before handover, we check coverage, edges, window reveals, transitions to the plinth and areas where the old surface may have absorbed paint unevenly. If scaffolding is needed, we plan it in advance so painting is not interrupted by lack of access.

Local experience and agreeing the scope of work

Preparation is what makes facade painting durable: washing, drying, removal of loose paint and the right primer affect the result more than colour choice. When assessing an enquiry, we always check site access, whether the space is occupied, which surfaces need protection and the most practical sequence of work. This helps avoid having to reopen or repair a completed stage later.

Before starting, we provide a clear, realistic estimate. If photos are not enough, we arrange a site visit. This lets us agree on materials, timing, related work and potential risks before the project begins.

Before facade painting, we also agree on colour, sheen, paint system and whether a sample area is needed. This helps avoid a technically coated large surface whose colour or coverage does not meet expectations.

Facade painting prices

Practical information about facade painting

Materials

Facade paint must be selected for the surface. Timber facades, render and plinths require different primers, paints and preparation; compatibility with the previous paint system also matters.

Time required

The time required depends on washing, drying, removal of loose paint, priming and weather. Good painting requires a dry surface and a suitable temperature.

Pricing factors

Costs are affected by surface height, access, number of paint coats, repair work, protection of windows and details, and whether scaffolding is required.

Common mistakes

Common mistakes include painting an excessively damp or hot surface, skipping washing and insufficient removal of old loose paint.

Advice for customers

Choose the colour using a sample area as well as the colour chart. On a large surface, a colour may look lighter or more intense than on a small sample.

Exterior paint is selected for the facade substrate

Timber: opaque paint or translucent stain

Opaque timber facade paint gives a more uniform colour; translucent stain leaves the wood grain more visible and requires different maintenance. Tikkurila Ultra Classic, for example, is an opaque timber facade paint. The type and adhesion of the old finish are checked before choosing a new system.

Mineral facade

For rendered surfaces, we select facade paint and primer compatible with the specific render and old coating. Silicate, silicone resin and acrylate paints differ in substrate compatibility and moisture behaviour. A new coat of paint alone will not resolve the causes of cracks or dampness.

Cleaning and primer

We remove dirt and biological growth with a suitable product, rinse and allow the surface to dry. We remove loose paint and repair damage. Exposed timber and unevenly absorbent mineral surfaces require the primer specified by the chosen paint system; timber impregnation and an opaque primer do not always serve the same purpose.

Painting conditions and quality checks

Colour sample and moisture

We approve the colour on different sides of the house and check that the substrate is dry. Painting conditions are determined by the product, not just air temperature: surface temperature, humidity and the weather forecast also matter.

Coats and drying time

We apply the coats required by the system, following coverage rates and recoating times. An excessively thick coat or painting a wet surface can damage adhesion. Cold, damp weather extends drying time; there is no universal number of hours for all exterior paints.

Edges and maintenance

We check end grain, board edges, window surrounds and junctions. Maintenance includes monitoring rainwater systems, biological growth and coating wear so small defects can be repaired before they become larger moisture problems.

For a quotation: Provide the house height, facade material and photos of the old paint condition. If possible, name the existing paint and desired colour. Scaffolding requirements and preparation are allowed for as separate work items in the quotation.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to paint a facade?

Dry, mild weather is best, with a surface that is neither wet nor overheated and no immediate risk of rain.

Does old paint need to be removed?

Loose and peeling paint must be removed. Not all old paint needs removing if it is sound and compatible with the new paint system.

Do you also wash facades?

Yes, we clean the facade before painting where needed.

How long does facade paint last?

Durability depends on the surface, preparation, paint system and weather exposure. Proper preparation significantly extends its service life.

Can a facade be painted immediately after washing?

No. The surface must dry sufficiently before priming and painting. Drying time depends on weather, material and the condition of the facade.

Is the same paint suitable for timber and render?

Usually not. Timber, render and plinths require different paint systems, and the wrong product may cause peeling or moisture problems.

How often should a timber facade be painted?

This depends on orientation, previous paint, the condition of the timber and maintenance. Sides exposed to sun and rain usually wear faster.

Does the weather need to be completely sunny during painting?

No. Strong sunshine can even be harmful, because the paint dries too quickly on the surface. What matters is dry, mild weather that meets the manufacturer's requirements.

Is completed work covered by a warranty?

Yes. Construction and finishing work completed by RK Meistrid OÜ is covered by a 3-year warranty. The scope of the work and agreed terms are recorded in the quotation.

Which other jobs is facade painting usually linked to?

Facade painting is usually part of a wider package of exterior work. If the surface needs repairs before painting, it helps to take a comprehensive approach to facade work; for higher walls, it is necessary to consider access and work safety. Around roof edges, rainwater systems or eaves, it may also be sensible to coordinate painting with roof maintenance or repair work.
