How should I use the completed projects page?

When looking at completed projects, pay attention to the type of work as well as the final result. A similar-looking result may require different preparation depending on the substrate, moisture, use of the room and materials. You can refer to a specific example that resembles your plans in your enquiry.

Descriptions of actual projects demonstrate experience better than a general list of services. Each added project strengthens the connection between a specific job, location and result.

The gallery also helps you assess the quality of work before sending an enquiry. If you want a similar result, mention which photo or style of work you like and which room or part of the building you want to renovate. This turns a comparison of projects into a practical starting point, as well as visual inspiration.

Descriptions of completed projects help distinguish small finishing jobs from full renovations in the future.

About our completed projects

If a project resembles your property, send photos and a description through the contact form. We can explain which stages are likely to be needed, whether demolition is required, which finish would suit the property and whether related services should be carried out in a coordinated sequence.

Can I request a quote based on a completed project?

Yes, an example helps identify the type of work, but the exact price depends on the dimensions, condition and materials of your property.
