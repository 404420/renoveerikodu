Bathroom tiling: the work process

Bathroom tiling in Tallinn is wet-room work whose result depends primarily on preparation. The tile itself is the visible final layer, but reliability is determined by the strength of the substrate, waterproofing, floor slopes, the area around the drain, tile layout, grouting and silicone sealing. The aim of this completed project was to achieve a clean visual finish and a wet room that withstands daily use.

Before tiling, we check whether the walls and floor need levelling, whether old tiles or sanitary fittings need to be removed and how to handle corners, niches and pipe penetrations. We then install waterproofing, plan the tile layout, lay the tiles and finish the joints and connections.

What should you send to request a quote for similar work?

For a bathroom tiling quote, the most useful information is photos of the current room, approximate measurements, details of old tile removal and the intended tile size. If the bathroom also needs demolition, plasterboard work, plumbing preparation or painting, include this from the outset. Large-format tiles, mosaic, forming floor slopes and complex corners affect the scope of work.

Plan related work in one coordinated sequence so that the substrate is ready before tiling.
