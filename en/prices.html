How should I use the price list?

The price list is intended to provide a quick guide before you submit an enquiry, rather than replace a site inspection. The same job can cost different amounts at different properties because the substrate, access, old finish, materials and schedule affect the scope of work. For the most accurate response, include photos, measurements and a description of the result you want in your enquiry.

If the job consists of several stages, such as demolition, plasterboard work, tiling, parquet installation and painting, it makes sense to request a quote for the complete project. This makes it easier to establish the work sequence and avoid having to reopen or protect a newly finished surface for the next stage. For more about preparation and pricing for flooring work, see the parquet installation service.

Price list FAQ

What does a starting price mean?

It is an initial guide for the work in a straightforward situation. The exact price depends on the condition of the property, materials, access and scope of work.

Do prices include materials?

Where the table has a separate “including materials” column, this is stated. In other cases, we specify the materials in the quote.

Can you provide an accurate quote from photos?

For smaller jobs, an initial estimate can be provided from photos. More complex work requires a site inspection.
