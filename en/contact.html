Request a renovation quote

We welcome enquiries about interior finishing, tiling, painting, drywall work, parquet installation, demolition, façade work, roofing and other renovation services in Tallinn and Harju County. For a quicker response, include the location, a short description, your preferred deadline and photos of the current situation if possible.

If you are unsure which service to choose, simply describe the problem or the result you want. We will help assess whether a site inspection is needed, which services are connected and what renovation sequence makes sense.

  • Tiling in bathrooms, kitchens and hallways
  • Painting and interior finishing in apartments and commercial premises
  • Facade work, roofing and seasonal snow removal

What does a good enquiry include?

A good enquiry includes the address or area, approximate dimensions, photos of the current situation, the desired result and information about whether the room is in use. If the work involves several stages, such as demolition, drywall, tiling and painting, mention this right away. This lets us account for the sequence of work, drying times and movement of materials when preparing the quote.

If you need the work done quickly, include your preferred deadline. If you already know your budget, you can mention it too so that we can suggest a realistic solution or divide the work into stages. The clearer the initial information, the more accurate our response.

Site inspection and clarifications

If the scope of work is unclear from the photos, we arrange a site inspection. An inspection helps clarify the condition of the substrate, access, material requirements and potential risks. This makes the quote more accurate and reduces surprises later on.

You can also send files and photos through the contact form. They help us better understand whether the job involves interior finishing, exterior work, structural repairs or several related services at once.

If the property is in Tallinn or Harju County, include the city district or locality if possible. This helps us assess logistics, a possible site visit and a realistic start date. Clear contact details also save time when quick clarifications are needed.

This contact page is for starting both small repairs and larger renovation projects. If you describe the problem as simply and honestly as possible, we can explain whether it is best to start with an inspection, clarify materials or divide the work into several stages.

Contact enquiry FAQ

How quickly do you respond to enquiries?

Response time depends on our work schedule and how detailed the enquiry is. Photos, dimensions and location help us understand more quickly whether a site inspection is needed.

Can you prepare a quote from photos?

For smaller jobs, we can give an initial estimate based on photos and a description. More complex properties need a more detailed inspection.

Can I order several services at once?

Yes. Ordering several related services helps us plan the sequence of work better and reduces downtime on site.
