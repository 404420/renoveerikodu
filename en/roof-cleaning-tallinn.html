Project description

Roof cleaning and maintenance help extend the life of a roof and identify problems before major repairs are needed. This is particularly important when dirt or moss accumulates on the roof, or gutters fail to drain water properly.

Initial condition

Dirt accumulated on the roof surface and blocked drainage routes can affect the roof, facade and rainwater system. Small problems may lead to more extensive repairs later.

Solution

During the work, the roof is cleaned according to the condition of the roofing material, visible penetrations and gutters are inspected, and recommendations are given for further maintenance or repairs.

Roof cleaning and maintenance: the work process

Roof cleaning in Tallinn is maintenance work intended to remove dirt, moss, leaves and other accumulated deposits before they affect the service life of the roofing material. Roof maintenance helps identify problems early: blocked gutters, areas around penetrations, loose fixings and damp spots may later lead to major repairs. For this completed project, we focused on cleaning the roof and inspecting visible areas of risk.

When planning the work, we consider the roofing material, pitch, access and safety. Different roofs require different cleaning pressures and methods, because excessive pressure can damage the roofing material or seals. We choose the cleaning method according to the material and condition, check rainwater drainage and explain honestly whether maintenance is sufficient or repairs should be planned.

What should you send to request a quote for similar work?

Include photos of the roof, gutters, access and building height in your roof cleaning enquiry. If you know the roofing material, such as metal, fibre cement, tile or bitumen, be sure to mention it. To refine the quote, also state whether you need roof cleaning only, gutter cleaning, roof maintenance, moss treatment or repairs as well.
